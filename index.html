About

Technical, and good with people

Hi, I'm Hallie. I like the part of software where the interface meets a real person.

Most recently I was Assistant Vice President, Software Engineer at Bessemer Trust, from Apr 2023 to Aug 2026. I helped modernize a legacy wealth-management client website and mobile app into cloud-based platforms. Where2P

Where2P finds a public restroom while you're out. Search a city or address, or use your location. Matches show up in a list and on a map, with accessible, all-gender, and changing-table details when the listing has them. You can read reviews and leave your own.

Refuge Restrooms API for listings, Leaflet and OpenStreetMap for the map, Photon to turn a search into coordinates, and Firebase for reviews. Built with Jake Dudum, Darryl Tolentino, and Adrienne Liu.
